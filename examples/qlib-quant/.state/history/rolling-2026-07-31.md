# Rolling-window study — 2026-07-31

The held-out result recorded on 2026-07-30 rested on one pair of segments. One
fold is an observation. This is five, and it says something the single fold did
not — including that the single fold was unrepresentative.

## Method

Five folds. Each trains from 2018 to the year before its validation year,
selects on the validation year, and is scored on the year after it, which no
part of selection ever saw. Both configurations are fitted once per fold and
scored on both segments from that same fit, through `run_qlib_backtest.score()`
so this study cannot drift from the single-fold runner.

```
python run_rolling.py
```

Raw output: [`rolling-2026-07-31.json`](rolling-2026-07-31.json).

## What it says

**The tuning reliably improves the metric it was selected on, and does nothing
measurable to the one it was not.** Every fold agrees on the first: five out of
five, mean +0.68, *t* = 5.94. None of them agree on the second: mean +0.03, *t* =
0.06, positive in three folds of five — a coin.

This is regressional Goodhart with a sample size. No one cheated in any of these
runs. The eleven-round sweep that produced λ=100/200 did careful, honest work,
and what it produced is a reliable improvement to a number and a null result on
the thing that number was standing in for.

## What it corrects

Both of this project's earlier readings of the single fold were wrong, in
opposite directions.

The first review called the +22.5% "a selection gain, not a discovery" —
too strong at the time, since one fold could not support it.

The 2026-07-30 record then softened that to "the direction transferred, the
magnitude did not", because on that fold the held-out figure moved from −1.1125
to −0.0297, an improvement of +1.08. With four more folds that reading does not
hold either: +1.08 was the third-largest of five fold differences whose spread is
0.91, and two folds move the other way. **The direction did not transfer. That
fold's apparent transfer was itself noise.**

The accurate statement, which four folds ago could not have been made: *the
tuning is a reliable gain on the selected segment and a null result out of
sample.*

The 2022 → 2023 fold is also the worst held-out fold in the study for the
baseline arm, at −1.1125 against a study mean of +1.10. Every alarm and every
softening in this repository's history was driven by one unrepresentative fold.

## What it still does not show

- **Five folds, one universe, one model family.** Not a distribution over
  markets, model classes, or feature sets.
- **The construction is unchanged**, and it is not qlib's backtest: top-30
  long-short, daily full turnover, no transaction cost, slippage or position
  limits. Every figure here inherits that. These numbers compare two
  configurations under one construction; they are not statements about tradeable
  performance, and the held-out column being positive in most folds should not be
  read as an edge.
- **The paired *t* assumes folds are independent.** Overlapping training windows
  make them correlated, which inflates the visible-segment statistic. The
  held-out result is null regardless, and null is the finding.
